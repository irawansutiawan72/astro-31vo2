# Asset credits

No external models, textures, HDRIs, or sound recordings were downloaded or included for this game. The playable course and its fallback character are generated procedurally in code; sound effects are synthesized with Web Audio.

The GLTF Draco decoder and Basis Universal transcoder copied into `public/assets/` are distributed with the project's installed Three.js package. Their corresponding Three.js license is copied alongside them as `public/assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

The game can optionally load user-provided Mixamo GLB character/animation files. These are not included and are not represented as CC0 assets. Obtain them through an authorized account and follow the applicable Mixamo terms. See [ASSETS.md](./ASSETS.md) for exact file paths and constraints.
